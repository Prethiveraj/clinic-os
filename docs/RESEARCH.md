# Design and SEO research

Reviewed 7 November 2026. These sources informed the implementation; their text and creative assets were not copied.

- [Eloqwnt](https://www.eloqwnt.com/): editorial hierarchy, oversized typography, rounded neutral surfaces and soft gradients informed the original platform/clinic designs.
- [Places Text Search](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/text-search): official API discovery and field masks, with explicit selection before fetching details.
- [Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies): transient discovery, place-ID retention and attribution. The Google Maps logo comes from the official attribution pack linked on this page. Automatic photo mirroring was removed; legacy records need operator review.
- [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide): clear site structure, crawlable internal links and meaningful titles.
- [Helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): patient usefulness and confirmed facts over mass-produced keyword pages. Word counts are not ranking guarantees.
- [Local business structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business): known visible clinic facts, assembled by the server instead of arbitrary AI JSON-LD.
- [Review snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet): no self-serving aggregateRating on the clinic's own site.
- [Groq models](https://console.groq.com/docs/models): retained the original provider and configurable GROQ_MODEL.

These practices do not guarantee ranking changes, rich results, medical accuracy or patient growth.
